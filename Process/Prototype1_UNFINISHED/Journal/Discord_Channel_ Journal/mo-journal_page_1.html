2/2 - Portal-In | Portal-Out: This mechanic embodied in randomly spawned portals, green ones you can trigger and red ones serving as destinations are simple spawn triggers that can help the player significantly shorten their path to the end at no step/turn cost, or lead them astray, costing them reaching the end or dramatically increase their step/turn count. The metaphor for the portals are previous biases, experience, reasoning that one may employ in reaching a moral verdict, and how such recall may aid in making holistically solid decisions more parsimoniously or, once again, misguide into not seeing the situation for what it is while relying on situating it within other known situations. - MazeEnd/Goal Statuses and Escape: The HUD represents various statuses based on the relationship between the player and the maze's end. Stationary denotes the player not moving or turning. Running is self-explanatory. Goal Reached means the player has reached the maze's end trigger within the allotted time. Wasteful triggers when the time is over, at which the MazeEnd trigger will start moving towards the MazeStart/Spawn point. Elusive Save is when the player is in Wasteful mode and manages to catch up and reach the maze's end before it reaches the spawn point. Finally, Singularity is when the maze's end reaches the spawn point, causing the situation to implode and the player to actually lose. The metaphors for these explore the various outcomes of engaging with a moral situation and what consitutes balance/good in this case. The counters freeze once the goal is reached, triggered, or singularity happens. What to do with the values is a WIP.

1/2 This is a small test on a 10x10 maze (I have tried it on bigger ones and it works, i can document that later but for the sake of brevity this is the test for now). Mechanics and Metaphors: - The Step and Turn Counters: This mechanic records player's navigation through monitoring the number of steps and turns based on orientation changes on an 8-dir system and also the velocity + displacement of player. The metaphor here is that steps and turns are either thoughtful contemplation or chaotic noise in the pursuit of moral verdict. - Time Countdown and Progress %: The time countdown serves to add an element of urgency and drive for th eplayer to engage with and inform their decision-making. The time is set according to a formula that takes into account the size of the maze and the number of junctions with degree > 1 (more than one path to follow). The progress "bar" calculates the proximity of player to the goal/end trigger for immediate feedback on decisions and performance (it can be misleading as wel which adds some interest). The metaphor to these mechanics is the reality of a situations positioning in space-time; moral dilemmas/challenges are not disconnected from the physics of existence. - Pivotal Points: This mechanic is a collectable that spawns only on maze junctions with more than one direction to enter. The PP adds 5 seconds to the timer which is a small reward for exploration, but will be counter-productive if exploration turns to chaotic noise as the time gained will not be enough to make up for lost time. The metaphor here for PPs is simply the dichotomy of accumulated experience vs. misguiding presumptions, our active decision to rely on indulging too much in mental olympics can help us in the grand scheme of things or simply confuse us.
